Every design decision in Agora exists to remove a gatekeeper. No one sits between you and the people you want to reach.

🔑

You Are Your Key

No email. No phone number. No verification selfie. Twelve words generate your identity — an Ed25519 keypair that exists only on your device. Nobody issues it. Nobody can revoke it.

🔒

Encrypted by Default

Every DM uses a fresh ephemeral key. Even if a relay is compromised, past messages can't be decrypted. Forward secrecy isn't a premium feature — it's the only mode.

🌐

No Single Throat to Choke

Your client connects to multiple relays across continents. Kill one — the others keep working. Kill them all — browsers talk directly. The network has no off switch.

📡

Peer-to-Peer Mesh

WebRTC data channels between browsers. Your posts propagate through the mesh without touching a server. The relay is a convenience, not a dependency.

✍️

Tamper-Proof by Math

Every post is content-addressed and cryptographically signed. Nobody — not a relay operator, not an admin, not a government — can alter what you said or forge your words.

📴

Your Data Lives With You

Everything caches locally. Post while offline — it delivers when you reconnect. Unplug from the internet entirely and your feed is still on your device, readable, yours.

How It Works

Trust math.
Not corporations.

Three steps. No sign-up form. No email confirmation. No phone number. No waiting for approval from someone who doesn't know you.

01

Twelve Words

Your device generates twelve random words. From those words, mathematics derives your identity — an Ed25519 keypair. Write them down. That piece of paper is your account, forever, on any device, anywhere.

02

Connect Everywhere

Your client connects to relays — simple servers that store and forward signed messages. They can't read your DMs. They can't edit your posts. They can't pretend to be you. Run your own if you want. Use ours if you don't.

03

Speak Freely

Post to topic communities. Reply to conversations. Send encrypted messages. Every action is cryptographically signed — verifiable by anyone, forgeable by no one. Your words carry your signature, not a platform's watermark.

The Truth

You are not the user.
You are the product.

Every "free" platform you've ever used made money from the same thing: you. Your attention, your relationships, your private conversations — packaged and sold. This is what that looks like side by side.

How it works today

✕ Your identity exists at someone else's discretion
✕ Your "private" messages are readable by the company, its employees, and anyone they share data with
✕ An algorithm decides what you see — optimized for engagement, not truth
✕ One policy change, one acquisition, one government request — and you're gone
✕ Your social graph, interests, and behavior are the product being sold
✕ You can't leave without losing everything

How Agora works

◆ Your identity is a cryptographic key that exists on your device. No one issued it. No one can revoke it.
◆ Messages are encrypted end-to-end with per-message forward secrecy. Not even relay operators can read them.
◆ You choose your topics, your relays, your community. No algorithm. No feed manipulation.
◆ Your key is yours. Take it to any relay. Run your own. Nobody can deplatform mathematics.
◆ Zero analytics. Zero tracking. Zero data collection. The code is open — verify it yourself.
◆ Your data lives on your device. Export it, move it, delete it. It was never ours to begin with.
