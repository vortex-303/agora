Features

Built different.
By design.

Every feature exists because centralized platforms took something from you. Agora gives it back.

🔑

Cryptographic Identity

No email. No password. No phone number. Your identity is an Ed25519 keypair derived from a 12-word phrase you control.

🔒

E2E Encrypted DMs

Every message uses a fresh ephemeral X25519 key. Forward secrecy per message. The relay sees only ciphertext.

🌐

Multi-Relay

Connect to multiple relays simultaneously. If one goes down, others keep working. No single point of failure.

📡

P2P Gossip

WebRTC data channels between browsers. Posts propagate through the mesh even when relays are offline.

✍️

Signed Objects

Every post, reply, and profile update is content-addressed (SHA-256) and signed (Ed25519). Tamper-proof by default.

📴

Offline First

Post while disconnected. Everything caches to IndexedDB. Outbox drains when you reconnect. Your data is always local.

How It Works

No accounts.
No trust required.

Agora replaces trust with math. Every piece of data is self-authenticating.

01

Generate Your Identity

12 random words create your Ed25519 keypair. Same words = same identity on any device. Write them down. That's your account.

02

Connect to Relays

Relays are dumb pipes. They store and forward signed objects. They can't forge, modify, or censor what they can't decrypt.

03

Post, Reply, Message

Every action creates a signed object published to all your relays + P2P peers. Topic feeds, replies, encrypted DMs — all cryptographically verified.
