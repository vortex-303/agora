P2P Heritage

Built on 25 years of P2P engineering.
No casino attached.

BitTorrent solved a problem in 2001 that most "web3" projects still can't: distributing data across millions of untrusted nodes without a central server. The core ideas — content addressing, gossip propagation, swarm resilience — are some of the most battle-tested engineering on the internet. Agora is built on those ideas. What we didn't take: the speculation.

🧬

Content Addressing

Every post is identified by its SHA-256 hash — the same principle behind torrent piece verification. Same content = same ID, anywhere in the network. No database. No central authority assigning identifiers. The data is the address.

🔄

Gossip Protocol

When a node receives a new object, it forwards to all peers. Objects ripple through the mesh like files through a torrent swarm. No coordinator. No bottleneck. Just propagation — the same protocol that made file sharing unkillable.

🛡️

Swarm Resilience

Kill a relay. Kill ten relays. The network keeps working because every browser has a copy and can talk directly to other browsers. This is the same architecture that made torrents survive two decades of legal attacks. It works because there's nothing to shut down.

The crypto industry took this same P2P technology and strapped a financial instrument to it. Tokens, mining, staking, airdrops — layers of speculation on top of engineering that already worked.

We took the engineering and left the casino.

No tokens. No mining. No "invest in the future of social media." The value of this network is the conversation happening on it — not a number on an exchange.
